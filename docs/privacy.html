Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026 · Applies to the DeskStats app for Windows

DeskStats is a desktop app that shows your PC's hardware readings (CPU, GPU, memory, storage, network and sensors) in widgets. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: DeskStats has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your hardware readings never leave your PC.

1. Information that stays on your PC

To work, DeskStats reads information locally from Windows and your hardware. This information is shown on your screen and is not sent to us or anyone else:

  • Hardware readings: CPU, GPU and memory load, clock speeds, temperatures, fan speeds, power, drive usage and network throughput.
  • Names of your hardware (for example your processor and graphics card), drive letters and network adapter names.
  • The names and memory use of running apps, if you turn on the "Top apps" list in the Memory widget.

DeskStats saves these files on your PC only, in %LocalAppData%\DeskStats:

  • Your settings and widget layouts.
  • A diagnostic log of the app's own events (for example "sensor driver installed").
  • hardware-report.txt — a list of the hardware and sensors detected on your PC. It is only shared if you choose to send it to us for support.

You can delete this folder at any time. Uninstalling DeskStats keeps it so your settings survive a reinstall; delete it manually to remove everything.

2. Optional online features

DeskStats only connects to the internet for the features below, and only while you use them. No personal identifiers, hardware data or usage data are included in these requests.

FeatureService contactedWhat is sent
Weather widget Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — geocoding, forecast and air-quality APIs The letters you type in the City box (to suggest matching places), and the chosen place's coordinates, to get the forecast and air quality. Not used if you don't add the Weather widget.
Public IP address (Network widget) ipify (api.ipify.org) A simple request that returns your public IP address so it can be shown in the widget. Not used if this option is turned off.
Sensor driver install (only when you click "Install sensor driver") The bundled, verified PawnIO installer (no driver download needed in version 1.0.4) The driver installer runs locally. DeskStats sends no hardware readings to the driver publisher.

Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address while they answer. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests. DeskStats does not send them anything else.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not create user accounts or ask for your name or e-mail.

4. Administrator rights and the sensor driver

DeskStats asks for administrator rights because Windows only allows hardware sensors (such as CPU temperature) to be read with them. The optional PawnIO driver is used for the same purpose. These rights are used only to read hardware sensors and to install that driver when you ask for it.

5. Children

DeskStats does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
